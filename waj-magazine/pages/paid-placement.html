Questions

What is the difference between paid placement and a free feature?

Paid placements are guaranteed — you select your package, pay, and we schedule your feature. Free merit-based submissions at wajmagazine.com/features are reviewed editorially by our team and selected based on story, impact, and fit. Free submissions are not guaranteed placement.

How quickly will my placement go live?

Digital Spotlight placements typically go live within 7–10 business days. Full Feature placements are scheduled with our editorial calendar and typically run within the next available issue cycle. Contact us for current availability.

Can I provide my own photos and content?

Yes. You can provide your own photos, bio, and content for review. Our editorial team will review and professionally format everything to match WAJ Magazine's standards before publication.

Do you offer refunds?

All paid placements are non-refundable once confirmed and scheduled. We work closely with you to ensure the final placement meets your expectations before publication.

How do I get started?

Click "Get Started" on your chosen package above or email us at magazine@wearejerseyent.com with your name, business, and the package you're interested in. Our team will follow up within 48 hours.
